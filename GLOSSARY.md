# Workforce Compass

Workforce Compass helps engineering offices organize their people and compare pay with internal ranges and external market data. This glossary records the terms agreed for the redesign.

## Language

**Office**

A named location within the organization to which people are assigned. An office may have a comparison labor market, and its roster can include people whose job level or base salary is still unknown.

**Unassigned**

The roster category for existing personnel whose office assignment has yet to be confirmed.

**Labor market**

The geographic benchmark used for pay comparisons. Multiple offices can use the same labor market.

**Career ladder**

An ordered sequence of job levels within a job family, shared across offices. A job family can have separate technical and management ladders.

**Job level**

A named step in a career ladder with a short code and a defined position in the sequence.

**Pay band**

An internal base salary range for a job level, with a minimum, maximum, and optional midpoint.

**Effective pay band**

The pay band that applies to a job level on a given date. A labor market override takes precedence while it is in effect, and the company band applies otherwise.

**Market benchmark**

External pay observations for a comparable job and market, with a source and effective date.

**Market dataset**

A named collection of market benchmarks for a labor market. A survey-derived dataset records its source and effective date.

**Survey job match**

A confirmed association between a survey job code and survey level and one internal job level.
